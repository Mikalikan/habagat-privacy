Privacy policy for Lagoon Rings

Last updated: 5 November 2026

This page describes the Lagoon Rings release build from Habagat Games: which data is involved, why, which other companies process it, and how to withdraw consent.

Who is responsible

Habagat Games is the controller for Lagoon Rings.

Contact: habagatgamesstudio@gmail.com

What the release build does

  • It stores progress and settings on the device.
  • It can show a rewarded video through Google AdMob, and only after the player chooses to watch it. The in-game reward is granted when that video finishes.
  • It can show a banner during play, after the new-player phase. The banner sits in its own area. It does not pause the round or cover the board.
  • It can show a full-screen interstitial only after a run ends (game over). An interstitial is never shown in the middle of a round.
  • During the new-player phase, only the voluntary rewarded video is used. That phase lasts until about 7 days have passed since install and the player has about 60 minutes of play. The banner and the interstitial wait until that phase ends.
  • Where Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent message is required, including in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, the game asks before it requests an ad.
  • It has no in-app purchases, no player account, and no reward for sharing.

Data that is processed

On the device

The game keeps the information it needs to run, including progress, settings, and the privacy choice, on the device. There is no Habagat Games account.

Advertising ID

The Android release declares the advertising-ID permission (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID). Google AdMob can collect and use the advertising ID to deliver ads, measure them, and limit fraud. The game’s own code does not read the advertising ID, and Habagat Games does not keep its own copy.

The advertising ID can be deleted or reset in the device settings. Withdrawing consent is described below.

Data Google may process for ads

When the game requests an AdMob ad, Google may process:

  • Device and app information, such as model, operating system, language, and app version
  • An app-instance identifier, or a similar identifier used by that Google product
  • IP address, which Google may use as an approximate location
  • Ad events, such as whether a rewarded video was requested or completed, whether a banner was shown, or whether an interstitial was shown after a run ended

The release build does not ask for a name, and it does not read contacts, photos, messages, or precise GPS location. If you email Habagat Games, the message contains whatever you choose to write.

Why this data is used

  • To run the game and keep progress and settings on the device
  • To show a rewarded video the player asked to watch, and to grant the in-game reward when that video completes
  • To show a banner during play after the new-player phase
  • To show an interstitial only after a run ends
  • To measure ads, limit invalid traffic, and operate the advertising SDK
  • To store and honour the privacy choice
  • To answer email sent to the address above

Habagat Games does not sell personal information. Where a consent message is required, ad requests that need consent are made only after consent. Refusing consent does not lock the rest of the game. A rewarded video, banner, or interstitial can be unavailable when an ad cannot be requested.

Third parties

Google AdMob serves the rewarded videos, the banners, and the interstitials. Google’s privacy policy, Google’s description of data from apps that use Google services, and the AdMob policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • AdMob policies and restrictions

Google may involve advertising partners as described in those policies. The release build does not add a separate advertising company besides Google AdMob.

Consent and how to withdraw it

Where the UMP message is required, the game shows that choice before an ad request. The choice can be changed later in the game: open Privacy options from the menu or settings.

On Android, the advertising ID can also be deleted or reset in the system settings. On iOS, if the game asks for permission to track, that permission can be refused and later changed in the iOS settings.

Children

Lagoon Rings is not directed at children. It is not offered as a children’s app and it is not in Google Play’s Designed for Families program. Habagat Games does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent personal information to us, email habagatgamesstudio@gmail.com and we will delete it.

How long data is kept

  • Information the game stores on the device stays there until the app storage is cleared or the game is uninstalled.
  • Habagat Games does not keep a server copy of progress, of the advertising ID, or of an account. There is no account to delete.
  • Google keeps AdMob data for the periods described in the Google privacy documents linked above.
  • Email sent to habagatgamesstudio@gmail.com is kept long enough to reply and to keep a record of the request, then deleted. That address is not added to a marketing list.
